# Asset sources

- Project covers: shared typography and palette with inline SVG charts approved by the owner. No generated illustrations are displayed.
- Ecommerce cover: horizontal revenue-share bars from `ecommerce-profitability-analytics/public/data/dashboard.json`, `products`, gross revenue grouped by category. Nutrition 4,078,653; Hydration 875,975; Wellness 499,202; Other combines Vitamins (466,208), Energy (425,488) and Accessories (266,016). Bar lengths use exact shares of this product-export total; largest-remainder rounding makes labels total 100% (62%, 13%, 8%, 17%). Synthetic data, not measured business impact.
- Fintech cover: overall application counts from `lendflow-fintech-analytics/web/src/data/dashboard.json`: 100,000 started → 45,692 submitted → 22,351 approved → 14,587 contracted → 13,065 funded. Column heights use these counts; percentages all use started applications as denominator. Curved bridges connect stages visually. Synthetic data.
- Energy cover and case page: official `apps/spike/data/monthly_pulse.csv` at Vaca Muerta repository commit `1c1c30eb9e29901048cbd812eb14f46c109e4439`. The available extract covers Jan–Dec 2025 only. The cover omits the year from its title; accessible descriptions and case limitations state the extract's scope. No live feed, extrapolation or 2026 coverage is claimed.
- Ecommerce and fintech case pages: real captures of public live demos taken 2026-10-01, separate from the new covers.
- dbt, DuckDB, Next.js, ClickHouse, Metabase, GitHub and LinkedIn: Simple Icons 13.21.0, CC0. Logos retain their brand colors with adjacent labels. DuckDB and ClickHouse use dark marks on yellow for contrast. Logos remain the trademarks of their owners. https://simpleicons.org/
- Meltano: official blue icon from https://github.com/meltano/meltano/blob/main/docs/docs/reference/images/icon.svg, downloaded 2026-10-01. Repo MIT license. Displayed with adjacent label.
- Favicon and OpenGraph: typographic compositions using the author's initials/name and the page palette, made for this portfolio.
- IBM Plex Sans: bundled via @fontsource/ibm-plex-sans, SIL Open Font License.

- Airflow: unmodified multicolor official SVG from https://airflow.apache.org/images/airflow-icon.svg, linked by https://airflow.apache.org/community/resources/. The single-color Simple Icons variant was replaced.
- Python: official two-snakes SVG from https://s3.dualstack.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/pythondotorg-assets/media/files/python-logo-only.svg, linked by https://www.python.org/community/logos/. A viewBox matching its original artboard was added for responsive sizing; original colors and artwork are retained.
- BigQuery: official color SVG from https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/google-cloud-legacy-icons.zip, linked by https://cloud.google.com/icons. Replaces the single-color variant with the recognizable official console icon and its original shades.
